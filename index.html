Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GPS Camera Pro ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

Important: GPS Camera Pro is primarily designed to process photos and location information on your device. The App does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

1. Information We Access

To provide the functionality of the App, we may request access to:

  • Camera: Used to capture photographs.
  • Location: Used to obtain GPS coordinates and location information for photo stamps.
  • Photos and Media: Used to save, access, or display photographs created or selected through the App.
  • Device Information: Certain technical information may be processed by third-party advertising services.

2. GPS and Location Information

GPS Camera Pro uses your device's location services to obtain information such as latitude, longitude, address, state, and postal/PIN code when available.

This information is used primarily to create GPS information stamps on photographs.

The App does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally maintain a personal location history on our servers.

Location information may remain embedded in photographs if you choose to save GPS information in the image or its metadata. Please consider this before sharing photographs publicly.

3. Camera and Photos

Camera access is required to capture photographs using the App. Photos created using the App are stored according to your device settings and Android permissions.

We do not claim ownership of your photographs. You remain responsible for the photographs and other content you create, store, or share using the App.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

GPS Camera Pro uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google. AdMob may collect and process certain information from your device to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on applicable settings, consent, and regional requirements, information processed by advertising providers may include:

  • Advertising ID or similar device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device and operating system information
  • Application information
  • Ad interaction and performance information
  • General diagnostic information

We do not control the data collection practices of Google or its advertising partners. Their processing of information is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

5. Personalized and Non-Personalized Advertising

Advertisements shown through Google AdMob may be personalized or non-personalized depending on factors such as your device settings, applicable laws, consent choices, and Google's advertising systems.

Where legally required, the App may request your consent before certain forms of personalized advertising or related data processing are enabled.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services necessary for advertising, diagnostics, or other functionality. These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

The primary third-party advertising service currently used by the App is Google AdMob.

7. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Core GPS camera functionality is designed to work directly on your device without requiring an online user account.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may rely on third-party service providers, such as advertising providers, that independently process technical information as described in their respective privacy policies.

We do not intentionally send your private photographs to our servers for advertising purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

GPS Camera Pro is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or registration system.

10. Your Choices

You can control or revoke Android permissions through your device's system settings. You may also manage advertising preferences through your Android device and Google's available privacy controls.

Disabling certain permissions may prevent some features of the App from functioning correctly.

11. Offline Functionality

Many of the App's core camera and stamping features are designed to work offline. However, advertising functionality may require an internet connection to load or display advertisements.

If an internet connection is unavailable, advertisements may not be displayed, while core offline features may continue to function.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, applicable laws, or third-party services.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date shown at the top of this page.

The App does not guarantee that GPS coordinates, addresses, timestamps, postal codes, or other location information will always be completely accurate.

Users should independently verify important location information before using a GPS-stamped photograph for legal, official, professional, surveying, property, safety, or other critical purposes.
