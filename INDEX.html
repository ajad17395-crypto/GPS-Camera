Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 22, 2026

Thank you for using GPS Map Camera. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

GPS Map Camera does not require registration or account creation.

The application may request access to:

  • Camera – to capture photos and record videos.
  • Location (GPS) – to display GPS information on photos and videos.
  • Photos & Media – to save and access captured files.
  • Date & Time – to display timestamps.
  • Device Sensors – such as speed or altitude, when available on your device.

We do not collect or store your personal information on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

The information is used only for the app's core functionality:

  • Capture photos and videos.
  • Add GPS information to media.
  • Display address, coordinates, date, and time.
  • Save files locally on your device.
  • Improve app performance and reliability.

3. Data Storage

All photos, videos, and GPS stamp data are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your media to any server.

4. Location Information

Location information is used only while the app is active to create GPS stamps on your photos and videos. Whenever possible, location processing is performed locally on your device.

5. Permissions

The application may request the following permissions:

  • Camera
  • Precise Location
  • Approximate Location
  • Photos and Videos
  • Storage (Older Android Versions)
  • Notifications (Optional)

These permissions are used only for the intended functionality of the application.

6. Advertising

This application may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Google may collect certain information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device Information
  • Approximate Location
  • App Usage Data

For more information, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

7. Third-Party Services

The application may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (if enabled)

The application does not use any paid GPS, map, or weather APIs.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

9. Children's Privacy

GPS Map Camera is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Since all captured media remains on your device, you retain full control over your data.

11. Your Rights

  • You may deny location permission.
  • You may deny camera permission.
  • You may delete your photos and videos at any time.
  • You may uninstall the application whenever you wish.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
